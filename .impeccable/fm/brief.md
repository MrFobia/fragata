# Surface brief — Fish Market (Inicio, Festival, Reservas, Eventos, Sedes)

Scope: fishmarket/index.html, festival.html, reservas.html, eventos.html, contacto.html. Mode: Persuade.
Visitor: Bogotá local on a phone from Instagram, planning a night out with friends during the festival. Action: book a table. Proof: the 30 real festival dishes (verbatim menu), real photos of Casa Fish Market 77 (brick house, blue awnings, terrace at night), dish photos re-edited with AI from the shared raw ceviches. Constraints: reservation and "¿Ya probaste?" simulated; WhatsApp secondary; no invented reviews or numbers; address Cra. 9 # 77-1 (client banner) for the restaurant, # 77-19 for the event house as the events HTML says.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The festival is announced the way Lima announces festivals: chicha posters pasted over each other on a wall, stacked lettering in two-ink fades, and between posters the night of the shared table caught with direct flash. Refuses the category default of a full-bleed dark food video with a logo and a card grid, and refuses cream editorial.

OWN-WORLD: Fish Market blue #223a84 owns the ground of every page (drenched). Poster inks: orange #da5f27 fading to cream #e9e2d1, sky #427ba2, green #8fa454 bands. Stacked condensed poster lettering for the three words of the festival (display, hand-built), Filson Pro role (Figtree stand-in) for headings, Noto Sans Canadian Aboriginal for text, Beth Ellen as hand-painted sign notes. Posters are paper slabs with slight rotation, torn/pasted edges, halftone grain; dish photos are flash-lit, hard shadow, cut out and pasted over the poster. Everything numbered in a fixed margin column (dishes 01–30, booking steps 1·2·3). No state is color-only: selection carries a stamp mark.

STORY: The visitor sees the festival poster first (CEVICHES · SUSHIS · BAOS, October and November, chef Carlos Accinelli), books or browses the 30 numbered dishes by category, rates one they tried, and finds the house on Carrera 9.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: logo top-left, menu right. A poster tilted -2deg fills ~75svh: "Festival de" in Beth Ellen, then CEVICHES / SUSHIS / BAOS stacked at full width in orange→cream fades, a green band "octubre y noviembre · chef Carlos Accinelli", and a flash-lit ceviche cut out overlapping the poster edge. Fixed bottom bar: orange "Reservar mesa" + "Ver festival". Desktop: poster on the left 7 cols, dish huge at right overlapping, tagline "Muchas formas de probar el mar" at the bottom.

FORM: Assigned grounded direction #5 of 7, "Cartel chicha del festival"; seed 02a88d33.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The paste-up. Posters enter by being slapped onto the wall (scale 1.04→1, rotation settle, a quick paper shadow), the stacked words re-fill their fades on scroll, and on Festival each category switch pastes a new poster header over the previous one. Dish numbers flip like a stamped counter. Reduced motion: posters are already on the wall, no slap, no flips.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
